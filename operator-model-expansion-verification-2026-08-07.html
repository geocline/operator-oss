Why final Hy3

Final Hy3 was selected instead of Hy3 preview. It is the newer July 2026 production release and advertises the broader tool-calling, structured-output, and sampling parameter set. The preview is cheaper—about $0.063 input / $0.21 output per 1M—but it would duplicate the same selector role with an older model. No newer same-vendor replacement for GLM 5.2 or MiniMax M3 was present in OpenRouter’s live catalog at implementation time.
